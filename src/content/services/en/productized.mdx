Productized automation flows and AI tools package a focused marketing task into something repeatable. They are intended for operators who need a practical starting point rather than a broad consulting engagement. Published products will include a clear use case, setup requirements and documentation, with availability shown in the Lab.

## What problem does it solve?

Some tasks benefit from a reusable workflow instead of a new process each time. A focused tool can provide a starting structure for that work.

## What do you get?

The published product’s documented workflow, setup requirements and usage instructions. Exact contents will be listed with each product before release.

## How does it work?

Browse the Lab to see availability and status. Products are published only when their use case and setup information are ready.

## What does it cost or how do we start?

No products are currently listed for sale. Contact me to discuss a use case. Pricing and purchase options will appear on individual product pages when available.
