Marketing consulting helps teams connect business priorities with acquisition, CRM and measurement. It is for B2B and B2C platforms that need a clear growth direction, a closer look at campaign performance, or practical support turning their marketing plan into work the team can carry forward.

## What problem does it solve?

Paid media, CRM and reporting can drift into separate activities. A shared review connects them to the same customer journey and business priorities.

## What do you get?

A review of your current approach, clear priorities and recommendations grounded in your data. The work draws on experience in insurtech, travel, retail and technology platforms across Southeast Asia.

## How does it work?

We start with your goals, existing campaigns and available data. Then we agree on the most useful scope, review the funnel and translate findings into an execution plan.

## What does it cost or how do we start?

Connect on LinkedIn or email a short description of the challenge. Scope and pricing are agreed after the first conversation.

## Common questions

### Can you work with an existing marketing team?

Yes. The scope can focus on strategy, a channel review or a specific gap alongside your team.

### Is this only for paid acquisition?

No. CRM, lifecycle communication and measurement are part of the same growth system.
