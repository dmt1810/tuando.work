Martech automation connects the tools, data and repetitive tasks behind your marketing operations. It is for teams handling lead routing, CRM communication or recurring reports through manual handoffs. The starting point is a useful workflow with clear inputs, ownership and a way to check that it behaves as intended.

## What problem does it solve?

Manual handoffs create extra work and make it harder to follow a lead or reconcile a report. Mapping the workflow makes the gaps visible before choosing an integration.

## What do you get?

A workflow map, an agreed integration scope and a plan for documenting the process. CRM integrations, lead routing and automated reporting are possible areas of focus.

## How does it work?

We review the existing tools and permissions, choose a focused workflow, define the checks and agree on implementation and handover. n8n is listed in the current skills profile and remains subject to the owner’s confirmation.

## What does it cost or how do we start?

Share the workflow you would like to improve. Scope depends on the systems, access and implementation needed. Pricing is agreed after a review.
